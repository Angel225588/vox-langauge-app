## App Store Description (4000 characters max)

### Short Description (First 3 lines - most important for visibility)

**Stop memorizing. Start speaking.**

Vox is your personal AI language coach that builds a learning path around YOUR reason to learn - whether it's landing that dream job, connecting with family, or ordering croissants in Paris.

---

### Full Description

**Why Vox is Different**

Other apps teach everyone the same lessons. Vox asks WHY you're learning, then builds a custom path just for you.

**Learning a language for your career?**
You'll focus on professional vocabulary, job interviews, and business communication - not "the cat is blue."

**Traveling soon?**
Get practical phrases for restaurants, hotels, and conversations - ready in weeks, not months.

**Connecting with family?**
Learn cultural expressions and emotional vocabulary that textbooks skip.

---

**Features That Actually Work**

- **AI-Personalized Paths**: Tell us your goal, level, and timeline. Our AI creates your unique learning journey.

- **Grammar Explanations**: Finally understand WHY "-ción" becomes "-tion" in English. No more guessing.

- **Speaking Practice**: Record yourself and get honest AI feedback on pronunciation - not just "Great job!"

- **12+ Learning Activities**: Vocabulary cards, fill-in-the-blank, sentence building, role-play conversations, and more.

- **See Your Progress**: Track exactly what you've learned. Every word. Every phrase. No hidden lists.

- **Works Offline**: Download lessons and practice anywhere - subway, plane, or beach.

---

**Languages Available**

- English (for Spanish & French speakers)
- Spanish (for English & French speakers)
- French (for English & Spanish speakers)

More languages coming soon!

---

**What Makes Vox Special**

Most language apps are games disguised as learning tools. You collect points, maintain streaks, but still can't order coffee in Spanish after 500 lessons.

Vox is different. We built it for people who actually want to SPEAK the language, not just tap through exercises.

No cartoon characters. No gamification gimmicks. Just effective, personalized learning that respects your time and your goals.

---

**Who Vox is For**

- Professionals preparing for international roles
- Travelers who want to do more than point and gesture
- Heritage learners connecting with their roots
- Anyone frustrated with one-size-fits-all language apps
- Intermediate learners stuck at the "plateau"

---

**Start Your Journey**

Download Vox today. Tell us your story. We'll build the path.

Because language learning isn't about collecting badges - it's about connecting with the world.
