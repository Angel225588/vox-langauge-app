# Marketing Skills Roadmap for App Founders

## Your Learning Path (1 Hour/Day)

This roadmap is designed for busy founders who need to learn marketing fundamentals while running their business. Each skill can be learned in 1-2 weeks with daily 1-hour sessions.

---

## Week 1-2: Copywriting Fundamentals

### Why This First?
Everything in marketing requires writing - ads, app descriptions, social posts, emails. Good copy = more users.

### Daily Schedule (1 Hour)

| Day | Topic | Resource | Time |
|-----|-------|----------|------|
| 1 | What is copywriting? | YouTube: "Alex Cattoni - Copywriting 101" | 1 hr |
| 2 | Headlines that hook | YouTube: "Alex Cattoni - How to Write Headlines" | 1 hr |
| 3 | Pain points & benefits | YouTube: "Copy School - Pain vs Pleasure" | 1 hr |
| 4 | AIDA framework | YouTube: "AIDA Formula Explained" | 45 min |
| 4 | Practice: Write 5 Vox headlines | Your practice | 15 min |
| 5 | Before/After/Bridge | YouTube: "BAB Copywriting Formula" | 45 min |
| 5 | Practice: Write Vox BAB ad | Your practice | 15 min |
| 6 | Call-to-actions | YouTube: "How to Write CTAs" | 30 min |
| 6 | Practice: Write 10 Vox CTAs | Your practice | 30 min |
| 7 | Review & apply | Rewrite all Vox ad copy | 1 hr |

### Free Resources
- **YouTube**: Alex Cattoni (100+ free videos)
- **YouTube**: Copy School
- **Blog**: copyhackers.com (articles)
- **Book**: "Ca$hvertising" by Drew Eric Whitman

### Practice Exercises
1. Write 10 different headlines for Vox
2. Rewrite competitor's app descriptions (Duolingo, Babbel)
3. Write 5 different versions of the same ad
4. Practice the "so what?" test on every sentence

### Key Concepts to Master
- [ ] Pain points vs benefits
- [ ] Features vs outcomes
- [ ] AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
- [ ] Social proof
- [ ] Urgency and scarcity
- [ ] Emotional triggers

## Beyond Week 10: Advanced Skills (When Needed)

### When You Have Budget ($500+/month)

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)**
- Build a blog for voxlanguage.com
- Target long-tail keywords
- Resources: Moz Beginner's Guide, Ahrefs YouTube

**Email Marketing**
- Build an email list from users
- Nurture sequences
- Resources: Mailchimp Academy (free)

**Influencer Marketing (Scaling)**
- Move from micro to mid-tier influencers
- Affiliate programs
- Resources: YouTube case studies

### When Hiring a Marketing Team

**Skills to Look For**:
| Role | Key Skills | Approx. Cost |
|------|------------|--------------|
| Paid Ads Specialist | Facebook/Google Ads, analytics | $500-2000/mo |
| Content Creator | Writing, design, video editing | $300-800/mo |
| Social Media Manager | Community, scheduling, engagement | $400-800/mo |
| Growth Marketer | Full-stack: ads, content, analytics | $1500-3000/mo |

**How to Evaluate**:
1. Ask for portfolio/case studies
2. Start with small project/trial period
3. Check references
4. Look for app marketing experience

## Mindset Tips

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Trying to learn everything at once** → Focus on one skill at a time
2. **Learning without applying** → Practice on Vox immediately
3. **Copying competitors blindly** → Understand WHY things work
4. **Giving up too soon** → Marketing takes time to show results
5. **Ignoring data** → Let numbers guide decisions

### Success Habits
- **Daily**: 1 hour learning, 30 min applying
- **Weekly**: Review metrics, adjust strategy
- **Monthly**: Assess progress, plan next skill
- **Quarterly**: Evaluate ROI, consider hiring help

### Remember
You don't need to be a marketing expert. You need to be good enough to:
1. Validate your product-market fit
2. Acquire your first 100-1000 users
3. Know enough to hire and manage marketing help

Good luck!
