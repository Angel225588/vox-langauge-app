Why Amber for "Work On"

Blue was wrong — Blue is Vox's brand color (#0036FF). Using it for "things to improve" sends mixed signals. The brand color should represent the product, not problems.
Amber is right — Universal "pay attention" signal. Warm, eye-catching on dark backgrounds. Not alarming (like red) but demands focus. Already in the Vox design system as colors.warning.DEFAULT.

Glass Effect — How

Gradient
160deg diagonal. Bright corner (10% color) fading to dark (4%).
Edge Light
inset box-shadow at top (15% color). Simulates glass refraction.
Border
1px at 18% opacity. Visible enough to define the container edge.
Glow
Outer shadow at 6% opacity. Subtle "floating" feel. Not harsh.

Mini-Chip Rules

Background: 5-6% of section color (darker than container)
Border: 1px at 10% of section color
Radius: 12px (smaller than container's 20px)
Min-height: 44-46px (fixed rhythm)
Gap: 6px between chips
