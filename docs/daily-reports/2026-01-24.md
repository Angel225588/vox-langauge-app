## Strategic Decisions Made

### Freemium/Premium Model

| Tier | Features | Price |
|------|----------|-------|
| **FREE** | 2 stairs + 5 voice calls (lifetime) + grammar | $0 |
| **PREMIUM** | Full staircase + unlimited voice + 9 accents + AI coach | $9.99/mo or $79.99/yr |

- Age: 31-50 (66.7% of language learners)
- Level: A2/B1 plateau
- Pain: "I know words but can't have real conversations"
- Why Vox: Grammar explanations + AI voice practice
