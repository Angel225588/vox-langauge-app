## 📝 DECISIONS MADE

1. **ComparisonCard Purpose**: Will be used for:
   - Homophones (their/there/they're)
   - Verb tenses (go/went/gone/going)
   - Formal vs Informal
   - Regional differences

2. **Feedback Personalization**: Tone adapts to user level:
   - Beginner: Simple, encouraging ("Like talking to a 10 year old")
   - Intermediate: Balanced explanation
   - Advanced/Professional: Technical, domain-specific examples

3. **Word Bank Expansion**: Can now store:
   - Single words
   - Phrases
   - Example sentences from lessons
