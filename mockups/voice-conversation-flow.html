Screen 3
Active Call
The core experience. ElevenLabs handles VAD (voice activity detection) automatically — no push-to-talk needed. The AI speaks the target language exclusively and responds quickly.
Avatar pulses when AI is speaking
Live timer with target duration indicator
AI responds FAST — no unnecessary pauses
Adaptive silence: Beginner 10s, Intermediate 7s, Advanced 5s before gentle nudge
AI ONLY speaks target language (absolute rule)
Key vocab is woven naturally into conversation
Red end button — always accessible
