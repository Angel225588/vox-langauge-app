Stage 2: Read Along
Host
Buenos dias, bienvenido al restaurante. ¿Tiene una reserva?
Guest
Buenos dias. No tengo reserva, pero somos dos personas. ¿Tiene una mesa disponible?
Host
Si, por supuesto. ¿Prefiere una mesa cerca de la ventana?
Guest
Si, por favor. Eso seria perfecto. Gracias.
Host
Muy bien. Un momento, por favor. Les preparo la mesa.

Stage 3: Understand
Host
Good morning, welcome to the restaurant. Do you have a reservation?
Guest
Good morning. I don't have a reservation, but there are two of us. Do you have a table available?
Host
Yes, of course. Would you prefer a table near the window?
Guest
Yes, please. That would be perfect. Thank you.
Host
Very well. One moment, please. I'll prepare the table for you.
